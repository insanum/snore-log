Snore Log privacy

Applies to the Snore Log iPhone app and the Snore Log web app. Last updated November 2026.

The short version

Snore Log does not collect, upload or share any data. Your recordings never leave your device unless you choose to share a clip yourself.

Microphone

Snore Log uses the microphone only while you have started listening. It measures sound levels to decide when to record, and saves short clips when it hears something louder than the room.

Where recordings are stored

  • iPhone app: in the app's own storage on your iPhone. They may be included in your iPhone or iCloud backup, like other app data, depending on your backup settings.
  • Web app: in your browser's storage for this site, on your device only.

Deleting a clip or a night in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes all of its recordings.

No accounts, tracking or analytics

There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code that collects data. The web app checks this site for a newer version of itself; that request sends nothing about you or your recordings.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock in the iPhone app is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Snore Log never sees your payment details.

Open source

All of Snore Log's code is public, so you can check any of the above: github.com/insanum/snore-log.

Not a medical device

Snore Log records sound. It does not diagnose snoring, sleep apnea or any other condition. Talk to a doctor about any concerns.
