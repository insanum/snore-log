# Snore Log

A one-page web app that listens overnight and records only when it hears a sound (like snoring).

- Measures the room's normal noise level, then starts a clip when a sound is clearly louder.
- Each clip includes the 3 seconds before the sound began.
- Stops after a chosen stretch of silence (1–5 minutes) and goes back to listening.
- Clips are listed by night with date and time, and can be played, saved or deleted.
- Recordings stay on the phone (browser storage). Nothing is uploaded.

## Use on iPhone

1. Open the GitHub Pages link in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
2. At bedtime, open Snore Log, tap **Start listening** and allow the microphone.
3. Leave the app open on screen with the phone on charge. Tap **Dim** to black out the screen.

iPhone turns off the microphone if the screen locks or you switch apps, so the app must stay in front all night.
