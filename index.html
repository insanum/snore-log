Tips for a good night's recording
  • Add this page to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) so recordings are kept safely.
  • Keep the phone on charge, about an arm's length from your head, microphone facing you.
  • Leave Snore Log open. iPhone stops the microphone if you lock the screen or switch apps. The app keeps the screen awake; tap Dim to black it out.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb so notifications don't wake you or show up in recordings.
  • If a fan or air purifier triggers clips all night, raise Sensitivity.
